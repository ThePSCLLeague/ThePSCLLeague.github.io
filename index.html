Import standings PDFRound results from the league report

Choose or drop the standings PDF and check the preview. Each player’s school, US Chess ID, rating, grade, round results and score are read from the file. The report is the league roster: players and schools that are not in it are removed when you import, so every leaderboard, standing, pairing and profile only shows the report’s field. Nothing is written until you press Import, which needs administrator sign-in.
